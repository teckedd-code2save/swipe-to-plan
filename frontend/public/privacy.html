Privacy notice · Updated 24 October 2026

Unstressed is a planning app operated by Serendepify. This notice describes the current hackathon application and the information used to suggest a day for you and your circle.

What you share

You can plan as a guest. The app uses the names, tastes, chosen planning area, party details, schedule and spending allowance you supply. A planning area is a place you select; the app does not automatically track your physical location. If you choose “Plan near me” or “Use my current location”, your browser asks for location permission. We round the coordinates to two decimal places on your device before sending them to our server and Qloo to suggest nearby planning areas. The device coordinates are not stored in your account, conversation, shared plan or usage analytics, and are not sent to the chat model. We do not continuously track your location. Only the area you confirm is used for planning; you can choose to remember that named area.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your verified Google account identifier, name and email address to create your account. We request only basic identity permissions: openid, email and profile. We do not request Gmail, contacts or calendar access, and we do not store Google access tokens.

How planning providers are used

  • Qloo receives artist searches, resolved artist and preference identifiers, and the selected area to discover places and cultural connections. The app also looks up selected venue identifiers to retrieve place details; schedule comparisons happen on our server.
  • When you use chat, Google Gemini receives your message, a short conversation history, the current planning brief, supplied participant preferences, saved circle summaries, people already connected through your circles (or guest profiles you supply), and bounded plan evidence. Avoid entering sensitive information about yourself or other participants.
  • Venue images load from their image providers. Maps links open an external maps service when selected. Those services process requests under their own policies.

Accounts, circles and shared days

People search for signed-in accounts is limited to accepted members of their circles. There is no public name or email directory. A private invitation link lets its holder sign in and choose to join a circle. Only share the link with people you want to invite.

Add to Google Calendar opens Google’s event editor with the outing title, venue addresses, schedule and group allowance in the link. It does not include participant taste profiles or private invitation links. You choose whether to save the event; Unstressed does not receive access to your calendars or automatically update saved events.

Signed-in tastes, circles and an optional usual planning area are stored on our server. A joined circle shows participants' names and preferences to its members. Add other people's information only when they are comfortable with you doing so.

Sharing creates a server-side day. Anyone holding its invitation or participant link can view the shared plan and tastes; different links carry different editing permissions. Treat these links as private. Shared-day access expires after seven days. Downloaded daybooks and calendar files are snapshots and remain wherever you save or share them.

Device storage and retention

When you’re signed in, generated plans save privately to your account, including their area, timing, people and taste connections. Revisions replace that saved day and retain one previous version for undo. These account days remain until you delete them from Saved days; they are separate from your default planning area and from shared-day links. Guest preferences, guest circles, optional usual areas and copies explicitly saved on your device use this browser's local storage. Saved days are separated by account but are not encrypted against other people using the same browser profile. You can remove saved days in the app or clear site data in your browser. The active conversation lives in browser memory.

Sign-in uses a necessary, secure, HttpOnly session cookie with a maximum lifetime of 30 days. Signing out ends that session. Account profiles and circles remain until removed or a deletion request is processed. The server may process technical request information to operate the service and limit abuse.

Product usage

We record basic first-party product events, such as planning requests, taste edits, opening place details, downloads, calendar opens and circle actions. A random identifier in session storage groups activity within a browser tab; signed-in activity can be linked to your account. The private owner dashboard shows account names, emails, sign-up dates and usage counts to help us understand and improve the product. Other users cannot access this dashboard.

Usage events do not include chat text, taste values, venue or location details, invitation credentials or IP addresses. Events are retained for up to 90 days or the latest 50,000 events. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. Browser Global Privacy Control disables optional feature-event collection; essential account and operational records remain.

Your choices and contact

You can edit your tastes, change or forget your usual planning area, remove saved days, and sign out. To request account access, correction or deletion, or ask a privacy question, contact createdliving1000@gmail.com. Include the email used for the account; do not send passwords, API keys or private invitation links.
