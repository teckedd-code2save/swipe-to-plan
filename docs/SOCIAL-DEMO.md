## Capture and edit

- Record a clean app-only window at readable text size. Hide browser sidebars and other accounts/tabs. Use a sample profile, with no invitation credentials or personal account dashboard visible.
- Keep one uninterrupted result/revision moment to demonstrate the working product. Cut or speed up waiting and repetitive clicks; do not imply edited waits are real-time responses.
- Use a 16:9 master for LinkedIn and YouTube. Make a separate vertical crop/re-record for Reels: show either the conversation or result at a time, not two tiny desktop columns.
- Record dry voiceover after the screen capture. Use short subtitles. Quiet, licensed music is optional; artist taste cards do not grant music rights.
- End with the live public app URL. Do not include private shared-day or circle invitation links.
- Create a separate 30-second “Bring your people” follow-up: accepted circle → tastes merge → day reworks → share. It deserves its own story rather than squeezing every feature into the solo demo.
